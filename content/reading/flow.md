A good read.

I was fascinated by this book ever since I had read that there was an author
that tried to systematize access flow state.<br>In fact, the main appeal of the
book is indeed the guidelines that it offers in allowing everyone into the state
of flow.

I have probably been at my happiest whilst in flow state but it had appeared as
a casual event, as if by magic. The books aims at explaining the way everyone
can tap into this kind of superpower.

### What I liked

I liked the scientific approach, the condensed explanation of the "optimal
experience" and hand holding that the author does in allowing everyone to
experience flow.

### What I didn't like

I liked a little bit less the way the author has tried to sum-up all the facets
of life through the lens of optimal experience. Thus, I found the main corpus of
the book bit tedious.

However, like many books of this kind, at the center lies a very straightforward
concept, that can be easily summarized and easily digested but the book, to
drive the point in, drags on and on on the same ideas.

I think a good summary could suffice.
