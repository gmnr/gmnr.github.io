This is my first book that was an **audiobook** instead of a book proper.<br>
(It's the first of many I fear, and so I have updated the site with a
self-explanatory icon, as there will be more audiobooks in the future)

A friend of mine suggested Rovelli, not really this book, but it was the
shortest I could find to test the new audiobook hypothesis (I have a quite long
commute, and I've ran out of interesting podcasts to listen to).

I've not really liked it, not because it's not a good book, but because I don't
think that the topic of the book was made to be listened to, rather than read
and pondered.<br>
Plus, the version I've got was read by Rovelli himself, and
let's just say, his wasn't the most perfect diction of all.

In the end, would I suggest it?<br>
If you are a physics super-fan maybe yes,
but none of the lessons went very far deep into the "meat" of the topic, it is
more akin a bird's eye's view of the modern theories (quantum, relativity).

Worth a shot.
